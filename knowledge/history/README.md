# History of steganography

Steganography as a field has three arcs worth carrying context for:

1. **Ancient → 1980s**: physical steg (wax tablets, invisible ink,
   microdots, punch-card patterns) into the first digital work.
2. **1983-2003 — Simmons through F5**: the academic founding of
   digital steganography. Prisoners' Problem, first LSB tools, first
   statistical detectors, F5 as the reference.
3. **2004 — present**: adaptive embedding, machine-learning
   steganalysis, Alaska2 competition, and the 2024–2026 AI-agent-era
   wave (Unicode tag prompt injection, hidden-instructions-in-images).

## Why this is Tier 4

The tactical KR (records + subtopic READMEs) captures what a
pipeline builder needs to design a working hide. History captures
what an assistant should know for context — the "why did the field
end up here" answer to questions like "why is F5 always the
reference?" or "why does OutGuess not survive today's detectors?"
It's for reading, not for playing.
